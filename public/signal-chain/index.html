Examiner note
Read these chains as examples, not formulas. Edexcel examiners explicitly criticise candidates who write "I would apply compression and EQ on every channel" without justifying it for the specific track. Processing should be reactionary — responding to what each track actually needs — and you must quote parameter settings (threshold, ratio, attack, release, cutoff, RT60 etc.), not just the effect names. Track-specific detail beats general points (2025 C3 PEF, verbatim).
