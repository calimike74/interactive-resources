Component 3 · AoS 3 — Development of recording and production technology

The history of recorded music, as a playlist

Five eras, told through the songs that changed how records get made. Every card is a first — the first time a machine, a technique or an idea is audible on a record you can play in the room.

Teacher selection, not spec content. The spec names no tracks, artists or producers — every choice here is a pedagogical one, and "first" claims in recording history are almost always contested. Cards marked contested first carry the standard account and name the rivals. Sits alongside the Era-ID listening drill (3.3), which trains the cues these songs demonstrate.

Every claim on this page is independently sourced and confidence-flagged — where a "first" is genuinely contested, the card says so and names the rival. Milestone songs are chosen from originals, not remasters, wherever the era's sound is the point.
